SUBJECT: INVITATION OF APPLICATIONS FOR STATE POST-MATRIC MERIT SCHOLARSHIP (ACADEMIC YEAR 2026-27)

1. PURPOSE & SCHEME OBJECTIVE:
Applications are hereby invited from eligible meritorious students currently enrolled in recognized Undergraduate (UG) and Postgraduate (PG) degree courses in universities and affiliated colleges for the grant of financial assistance under the State Merit Scholarship Scheme for Academic Year 2026-27.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Candidate must be a bonafide resident of Uttar Pradesh.
- Must have secured minimum 65% aggregate marks (or 6.5 CGPA) in the previous qualifying annual examination.
- Candidate must be enrolled full-time in an accredited college/university for session 2026-27.

3. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS TO BE ATTACHED:
a) Self-attested photocopy of previous qualifying examination marksheet
b) College Bonafide Student Identity Card copy
c) Aadhaar Card copy (UIDAI verified)
d) Bank Passbook first page displaying IFSC code and active account number

4. SUBMISSION DEADLINE:
All complete application dossiers must reach the scholarship cell before sunset on 15/11/2026. Late submissions will be rejected without consideration.

5. ACTION REQUIRED & SUBMISSION PROCEDURE:
Download standard Form-A from university portal (www.uphe-scholarship.gov.in), obtain verification seal from College Dean, and submit physical dossier in duplicate to Room 104, Administrative Block.

6. HELPDESK & CONTACT DETAILS:
Scholarship Cell Helpline: 0522-2239401 / 0522-2239402 | Official Email: helpdesk.scholarship@uphe.gov.in
Office Hours: 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM (Monday to Friday)
